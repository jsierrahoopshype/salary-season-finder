Highest-paid players of the 2005 NBA draft

Nobody from the 2005 draft has been paid more than Chris Paul, $404.3 million through 2025-26, more than double what anyone else on the list has earned.

His $41.4 million in 2020-21 is the biggest single season on the list too.

One player from the 2005 class is still being paid: Tyrese Proctor, $2.2 million in 2026-27.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
