Highest-paid players of the 2008 NBA draft

Russell Westbrook leads all players from the 2008 draft class with $348.3 million across 17 seasons.

His $47.1 million in 2022-23 is also the biggest single season on the list.

In 2026-27 the highest-paid player from the 2008 draft class is Brook Lopez at $9.2 million.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
