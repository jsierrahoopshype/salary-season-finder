Highest-paid players of the 2010 NBA draft

Paul George has earned more than any other player from the 2010 draft class, $462.1 million across 17 seasons to date.

His $54.1 million in 2026-27 is also the biggest single season on the list.

Paul George's contract would pay him $56.6 million in 2027-28, more than any player from the 2010 draft class has earned in a season.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
