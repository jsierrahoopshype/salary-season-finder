Highest-paid players of the 2021 NBA draft

Cade Cunningham has earned more than any other player from the 2021 draft class, $142.1 million across 6 seasons to date.

His $50.1 million in 2026-27 is also the biggest single season on the list.

Cade Cunningham's contract would pay him $53.8 million in 2027-28, $57.5 million in 2028-29 and $61.2 million in 2029-30, each more than any player from the 2021 draft class has earned in a season.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
