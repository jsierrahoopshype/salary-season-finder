Highest-paid players of the 2016 NBA draft

No player from the 2016 class has made more in the NBA than Jaylen Brown, at $294.9 million through 2026-27.

That $57.7 million in 2026-27 is the biggest one year any player from the 2016 class has had.

Ahead of him sit $61.7 million in 2027-28 and $65.6 million in 2028-29.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
