Highest-paid Texas players in the NBA

No Texas player has been paid more in one year than $54.7 million, which Kevin Durant drew in 2025-26.

His career earnings are the highest of them too, $555.7 million through 2026-27, more than double what any other player on this list has earned.

In 2026-27 he is the best paid of them once more, at $43.9 million.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
