Highest-paid Stanford players in the NBA

Brook Lopez owns the program's biggest single season, $25 million in 2023-24.

His $238.7 million through 2026-27 leads Stanford players as well. That is more than double what any other Stanford player has earned.

He is still the best-paid player on this list in 2026-27, at $9.2 million.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
