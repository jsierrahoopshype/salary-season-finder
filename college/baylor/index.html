Highest-paid Baylor players in the NBA

No Baylor player has been paid more in one year than $13.3 million, which Taurean Prince drew in 2021-22.

Royce O'Neale tops this list with $70.2 million through 2026-27.

The biggest season is still ahead: Keyonte George is due $28.7 million in 2027-28, $29.9 million in 2028-29 and $31 million in 2029-30.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
