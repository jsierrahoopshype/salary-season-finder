Highest-paid Illinois players in the NBA

The biggest single season for a Illinois player belongs to Deron Williams, $19.8 million in 2014-15.

His career earnings are the highest of them too, $152 million through 2018-19, more than double what any other player on this list has earned.

No Illinois player has been paid what Ayo Dosunmu is owed, $20.9 million in 2027-28, $22.4 million in 2028-29 and $23.9 million in 2029-30.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
