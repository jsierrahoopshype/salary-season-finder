Highest-paid Seton Hall players in the NBA

Samuel Dalembert leads all Seton Hall players with $89.6 million across 15 seasons. That is more than three times what any other Seton Hall player has earned.

His $13.4 million in 2010-11 is also the biggest single season on the list.

In 2026-27 the highest-paid Seton Hall player is Sandro Mamukelashvili at $13 million.

Sandro Mamukelashvili's contract would pay him $12.3 million in 2027-28, $13 million in 2028-29 and $13.7 million in 2029-30, each more than any Seton Hall player has earned in a season.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
