Highest-paid Arkansas players in the NBA

Joe Johnson has drawn the biggest salary of any Arkansas player, $23.2 million in 2014-15.

His career earnings are the highest of them too, $217.5 million through 2021-22, more than double what any other player on this list has earned.

In 2026-27 the best-paid Arkansas player is Daniel Gafford, at $17.3 million.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
