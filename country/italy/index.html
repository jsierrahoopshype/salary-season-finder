Highest-paid NBA players from Italy

Danilo Gallinari leads all players from Italy with $205 million across 16 seasons. That is more than double what any other player from Italy has earned.

His $22.6 million in 2019-20 is also the biggest single season on the list.

In 2026-27 the highest-paid player from Italy is Simone Fontecchio at $2.6 million.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
