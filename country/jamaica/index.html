Highest-paid NBA players from Jamaica

Roy Hibbert leads all players from Jamaica with $70.7 million across 9 seasons. That is more than three times what any other player from Jamaica has earned.

His $15.5 million in 2015-16 is also the biggest single season on the list. That is more than three times what any other player from Jamaica has been paid for a season.

In 2026-27 the highest-paid player from Jamaica is Josh Minott at $4.5 million.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
