Highest-paid NBA players from Senegal

Gorgui Dieng leads all players from Senegal with $77.8 million across 10 seasons.

His $17.6 million in 2020-21 is also the biggest single season on the list. That is more than double what any other player from Senegal has been paid for a season.

In 2026-27 the highest-paid player from Senegal is Tacko Fall at $2.5 million.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
