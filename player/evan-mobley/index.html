What the numbers say

Evan Mobley has been the Cavaliers' highest-paid player every season since 2025-26. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $133.2 million, the second-most of any player from the 2021 draft class. His $61.2 million in 2029-30 would be the biggest single-season salary of any center.
