    What the numbers say

    Anthony Davis has been the league's highest-paid Kentucky player every season since 2025-26. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $424.1 million, more than any other player from the 2012 draft class. He is third in career earnings among players who have not won MVP. His $62.8 million in 2027-28 would be the biggest single-season salary of any player from the United States.
