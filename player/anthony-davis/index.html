    What the numbers say

    Anthony Davis is the highest-paid player from the 2012 draft class ever, both in a single season ($58.5 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($424.1 million through 2026-27). Both are also records for a Kentucky player, and his 2026-27 salary is the highest ever for a No. 1 pick. Devin Booker is contracted to pass the single-season mark for a Kentucky player in 2028-29. He has been the Wizards' highest-paid player every season since 2025-26.
