    What the numbers say

    Kemba Walker is the highest-paid Connecticut player ever, both in a single season ($37.3 million in 2022-23) and in career earnings ($198.4 million through 2022-23). His 2022-23 salary is also the highest ever for a No. 9 pick. He was the Celtics' highest-paid player in 2019-20 and 2020-21.
