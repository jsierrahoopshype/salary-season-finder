    What the numbers say

    Ben Simmons is the highest-paid player from Oceania ever, both in a single season ($40.3 million in 2024-25) and in career earnings ($207.4 million through 2026-27). His 2024-25 salary is also the highest ever for an LSU player. He was the league's highest-paid player from Australia in every season from 2020-21 to 2024-25.
