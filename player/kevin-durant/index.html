    What the numbers say

    Kevin Durant is the highest-paid No. 2 pick ever, both in a single season ($54.7 million in 2025-26) and in career earnings ($555.7 million through 2026-27). Both are also records for a player from the 2007 draft class or a Texas player. He was the league's highest-paid forward in every season from 2023-24 to 2025-26.
