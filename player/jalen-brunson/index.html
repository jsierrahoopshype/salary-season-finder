    What the numbers say

    Jalen Brunson is the highest-paid No. 33 pick ever, both in a single season ($37.7 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($157.8 million through 2026-27). His 2026-27 salary is also the highest ever for a Villanova player. He has been the league's highest-paid Villanova guard every season since 2025-26. His $43.3 million in 2028-29 would be the biggest single-season salary of any No. 33 pick.
