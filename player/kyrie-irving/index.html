    What the numbers say

    Kyrie Irving was the league's highest-paid Duke player in every season from 2019-20 to 2022-23. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $391.9 million, 3 times as much as the next Duke guard, JJ Redick. He is seventh in career earnings among players who have not won MVP. His $42.4 million in 2027-28 would be the biggest single-season salary in Mavericks history.
