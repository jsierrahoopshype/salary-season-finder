    What the numbers say

    Kyrie Irving's $391.9 million in career earnings through 2026-27 is the most ever by a Duke player. He was the league's highest-paid Duke guard in every season from 2019-20 to 2022-23. He is seventh in career earnings among players who have not won MVP. His $42.4 million in 2027-28 would be the biggest single-season salary in Mavericks history.
