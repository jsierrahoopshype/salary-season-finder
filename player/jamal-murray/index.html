    What the numbers say

    Jamal Murray has been the league's highest-paid player from Canada every season since 2023-24. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $269.3 million, the second-most of any No. 7 pick. His $57.5 million in 2028-29 would be the biggest single-season salary of any Kentucky guard.
