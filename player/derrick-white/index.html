    What the numbers say

    Derrick White is the highest-paid Colorado player ever, both in a single season ($30.3 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($138.7 million through 2026-27). He was the league's highest-paid No. 29 pick in 2021-22 and 2022-23. His $34.8 million in 2028-29 would be the biggest single-season salary of any Colorado player.
