    What the numbers say

    Dejounte Murray is the highest-paid No. 29 pick ever, both in a single season ($32.8 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($164.5 million through 2026-27). Both are also records for a Washington player. Derrick White is contracted to pass the single-season mark for a No. 29 pick in 2028-29, and Jaden McDaniels the one for a Washington player in 2029-30.
