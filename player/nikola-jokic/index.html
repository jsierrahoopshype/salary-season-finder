    What the numbers say

    Nikola Jokic has been the Nuggets' highest-paid player every season since 2021-22. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $364 million, 5 times as much as the next No. 41 pick, Cuttino Mobley. His $62.8 million in 2027-28 would be the biggest single-season salary of any second-round pick.
