    What the numbers say

    Nikola Jokic is the highest-paid No. 41 pick ever, both in a single season ($59 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($364 million through 2026-27). His 2026-27 salary is also the highest ever for a player from the 2014 draft class, an international player or a center, and his career total is the most ever by a Serbian player. Joel Embiid is contracted to pass the single-season mark for a player from the 2014 draft class or a center in 2028-29, and Shai Gilgeous-Alexander the one for an international player in 2028-29. He has been the Nuggets' highest-paid player every season since 2021-22.
