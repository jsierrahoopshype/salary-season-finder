    What the numbers say

    Hakeem Olajuwon's $16.7 million in 2000-01 is the highest single-season salary ever paid to a Houston player. He earned $103 million through 2003-04.

    Season by season, what changed

    Only the seasons that changed something, newest first.

    2003-04
    Became the league's highest-paid player from the 1984 draft class.
    2001-02
    John Stockton took over as the league's highest-paid player from the 1984 draft class.
    2000-01
    Became the biggest career earner among players from the 1984 draft class.
    1998-99
    Took back the league's highest-paid player from the 1984 draft class spot.
    1997-98
    At $11.2 million he was the best-paid Houston player the league had seen, and a Rockets record.
    1996-97
    Lost the single-season record among players from the 1984 draft class to Michael Jordan.
    1995-96
    Set a new high for a player from the 1984 draft class, $5.3 million.
    1994-95
    Moved up to third among centers in career earnings.
    1993-94
    Won his first MVP while No. 3 in career earnings among players who had not won one.
    1992-93
    Dropped to fourth among centers in career earnings.
    1991-92
    Lost the single-season record among players from the 1984 draft class to Michael Jordan. Made his first All-Star team while No. 2 in career earnings among players who had not made one.
    1990-91
    His $3.2 million was the most ever paid to a player from the 1984 draft class and a Rockets record. Peaked at No. 2 among players who had not made an All-Star team.
