    What the numbers say

    Tobias Harris is the highest-paid No. 19 pick ever, both in a single season ($39.3 million in 2023-24) and in career earnings ($318.1 million through 2026-27). Both are also records for a Tennessee player. Brandin Podziemski is contracted to pass the single-season mark for a No. 19 pick in 2030-31. He was the 76ers' highest-paid player in every season from 2019-20 to 2022-23.
