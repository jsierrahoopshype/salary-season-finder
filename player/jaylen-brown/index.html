What the numbers say

Jaylen Brown has been the highest-paid player from the 2016 draft class in the league every season since 2024-25. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $294.9 million, 33% of all the money ever paid to players out of California. His $65.6 million in 2028-29 would be the biggest single-season salary of any player from the United States.
