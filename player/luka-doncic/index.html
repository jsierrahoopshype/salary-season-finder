    What the numbers say

    Luka Doncic has been the league's highest-paid player from Slovenia every season since 2022-23. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $249.7 million, more than any other player from the 2018 draft class. His $59.2 million in 2028-29 would be the biggest single-season salary of any international player.
