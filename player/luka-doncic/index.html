    What the numbers say

    Luka Doncic is the highest-paid player from the 2018 draft class ever, both in a single season ($51 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($249.7 million through 2026-27). Both are also records for a Slovenian player. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander is contracted to pass the single-season mark for a player from the 2018 draft class in 2027-28. He was the Mavericks' highest-paid player in 2022-23 and 2023-24.
