    What the numbers say

    Cade Cunningham has been the Pistons' highest-paid player every season since 2025-26. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $142.1 million, more than any other player from the 2021 draft class. His $61.2 million in 2029-30 would be the biggest single-season salary of any No. 1 pick.
