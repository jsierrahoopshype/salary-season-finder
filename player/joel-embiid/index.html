    What the numbers say

    Joel Embiid is the highest-paid Kansas player ever, both in a single season ($57.7 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($379.7 million through 2026-27). His career total is also the most ever by a player from the 2014 draft class or a center. He has been the 76ers' highest-paid player every season since 2023-24. His $67 million in 2028-29 would be the biggest single-season salary of any player from the United States.
