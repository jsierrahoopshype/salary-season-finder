What the numbers say

Season by season, newest first.

2023-24

Gordon Hayward's $33.3 million in 2023-24 was the highest single-season salary by a player who never made an All-NBA team, ahead of Paul Millsap's $30.8 million (2017-18). He had earned $272.7 million through 2023-24, the most of any No. 9 pick.

2022-23

Gordon Hayward was paid $30.1 million in 2022-23, the most in Hornets history.

2021-22

In 2021-22 Gordon Hayward was paid $29.9 million, the most in Hornets history. He passed $200 million in career earnings in 2021-22.

2020-21

In 2020-21 Gordon Hayward was paid $28.5 million, the most in Hornets history.

2019-20

Gordon Hayward's $32.7 million in 2019-20 was the highest single-season salary by a player who never made an All-NBA team, ahead of Paul Millsap's $30.8 million (2017-18). He passed $150 million in career earnings in 2019-20.

2018-19

In 2018-19 Gordon Hayward was paid $31.2 million, the most in Celtics history and the most in the 2010 draft class.

2017-18

In 2017-18 Gordon Hayward was paid $29.7 million, the most in Celtics history, the most of any No. 9 pick and the most in the 2010 draft class.

2014-15

Gordon Hayward was the Jazz's highest-paid player in 2014-15 after ranking seventh on the roster in 2013-14.
