What the numbers say

De'Aaron Fox has been the Spurs' highest-paid player every season since 2025-26. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $237.1 million, the third-most of any player from the 2017 draft class, behind Jayson Tatum and Donovan Mitchell. His $61.4 million in 2029-30 would be the biggest single-season salary of any Kentucky player.
