Season by season, what changed

Only the seasons that changed something, newest first.

2003-04
Was passed as the highest-paid No. 13 pick by Kobe Bryant. Took the biggest cut in the league, $17.8 million.
2002-03
Passed $100 million in career earnings. Dropped to third on the list of highest-paid forwards ever.
2000-01
Became the highest-paid player from the 1985 draft class. Crossed $50 million in career earnings.
1999-00
Set the biggest single-season salary ever paid to a No. 13 pick, $14 million. Reached the NBA's top 10 salaries for the first time, eighth.
1998-99
Pushed the Jazz single-season record to $6.1 million. Took over as the highest-paid No. 13 pick.
1997-98
Became the highest-paid player on the Jazz.
1996-97
Saw John Stockton pass his Jazz single-season record. Moved up to fifth among forwards in career earnings.
1994-95
Lost the single-season high among No. 13 picks to Dale Davis. Moved up to fourth among players from the 1985 draft class in career earnings.
1992-93
Dropped to fifth on the list of highest-paid players from the 1985 draft class ever.
1991-92
Climbed to fourth on the list of highest-paid players from the 1985 draft class ever.
1990-91
Set the biggest single-season salary in Jazz history, $2.3 million. Became the first No. 13 pick paid $2.3 million in a season.
