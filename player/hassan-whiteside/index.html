    What the numbers say

    Hassan Whiteside was the league's highest-paid No. 33 pick in every season from 2016-17 to 2019-20. He earned $105.4 million through 2021-22.

    Season by season, what changed

    Only the seasons that changed something, newest first.

    2019-20
    Crossed $100 million in career earnings, fifth among players from the 2010 draft class.
    2018-19
    Passed $50 million in career earnings.
    2017-18
    Gordon Hayward took over the single-season high among players from the 2010 draft class.
    2016-17
    At $22.1 million he was the best-paid player from the 2010 draft class the league had seen, and the most ever for a player drafted in the second round.
