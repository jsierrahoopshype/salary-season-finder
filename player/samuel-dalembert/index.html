    What the numbers say

    Samuel Dalembert is the highest-paid Seton Hall player ever, both in a single season ($13.4 million in 2010-11) and in career earnings ($89.6 million through 2015-16). Sandro Mamukelashvili is contracted to pass the single-season mark in 2029-30.
