    What the numbers say

    Domantas Sabonis is the highest-paid Gonzaga player ever, both in a single season ($45.5 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($228.2 million through 2026-27). Both are also records for a Lithuanian player, and his 2026-27 salary is the highest ever for a No. 11 pick. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander is contracted to pass the single-season mark for a No. 11 pick in 2027-28, and Chet Holmgren the one for a Gonzaga player in 2029-30. His $48.6 million in 2027-28 would be the biggest single-season salary of any No. 11 pick.
