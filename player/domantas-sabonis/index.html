What the numbers say

Domantas Sabonis has been the league's highest-paid Gonzaga player every season since 2022-23. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $228.2 million, 34% of all the money ever paid to players from Lithuania. His $48.6 million in 2027-28 would be the biggest single-season salary of any No. 11 pick.
