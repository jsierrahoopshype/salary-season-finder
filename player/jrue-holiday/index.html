    What the numbers say

    Jrue Holiday is the highest-paid No. 17 pick ever, both in a single season ($37.6 million in 2023-24) and in career earnings ($357.4 million through 2026-27). His career total is also the most ever by a UCLA player. He was the Pelicans' highest-paid player in 2017-18 and 2018-19. He has earned more than any player who has not made an All-NBA team.
