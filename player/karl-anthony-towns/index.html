    What the numbers say

    Karl-Anthony Towns is the highest-paid Latin American player ever, both in a single season ($57.1 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($343.4 million through 2026-27). His career total is also the most ever by a player from the 2015 draft class. He has been the Knicks' highest-paid player every season since 2024-25. He is 10th in career earnings among players who have not won MVP.
