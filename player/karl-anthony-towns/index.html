What the numbers say

Karl-Anthony Towns has been the highest-paid Latin American player every season since 2020-21. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $343.4 million, more than any other player from the 2015 draft class. His $61 million in 2027-28 would be the biggest single-season salary of any international player.
