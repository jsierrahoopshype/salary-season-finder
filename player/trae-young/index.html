    What the numbers say

    Trae Young has been the league's highest-paid No. 5 pick every season since 2022-23. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $242.6 million, the second-most of any Oklahoma player. His $58.4 million in 2029-30 would be the biggest single-season salary of any player from the 2018 draft class.
