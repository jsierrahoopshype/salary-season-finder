    What the numbers say

    Kyle Lowry is the highest-paid No. 24 pick ever, both in a single season ($33.3 million in 2019-20) and in career earnings ($282.1 million through 2025-26). Both are also records for a player from the 2006 draft class, and his career total is the most ever by a Villanova player. He was the Raptors' highest-paid player in every season from 2017-18 to 2019-20.
