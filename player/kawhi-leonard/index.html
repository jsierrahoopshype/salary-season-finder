    What the numbers say

    Kawhi Leonard's $428.8 million in career earnings through 2026-27 is the most ever by a No. 15 pick or a player from the 2011 draft class. He was the Clippers' highest-paid player in every season from 2022-23 to 2025-26. He is second in career earnings among players who have not won MVP. His $58.9 million in 2028-29 would be the biggest single-season salary of any forward.
