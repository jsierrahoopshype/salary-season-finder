    What the numbers say

    Kristaps Porzingis is the highest-paid Latvian player ever, both in a single season ($36 million in 2023-24) and in career earnings ($256.9 million through 2026-27). He was the Mavericks' highest-paid player in 2019-20 and 2020-21. He is 10th in career earnings among players who have not made an All-NBA team.
