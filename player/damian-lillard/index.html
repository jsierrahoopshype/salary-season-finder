    What the numbers say

    Damian Lillard is the highest-paid No. 6 pick ever, both in a single season ($48.8 million in 2024-25) and in career earnings ($402.5 million through 2026-27). He was the Trail Blazers' highest-paid player in every season from 2017-18 to 2022-23. He is sixth in career earnings among players who have not won MVP.
