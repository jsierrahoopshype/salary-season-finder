    What the numbers say

    Damian Lillard was the Trail Blazers' highest-paid player in every season from 2016-17 to 2022-23. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $425.1 million, more than any other player from the 2012 draft class. He is third in career earnings among players who have not won MVP.
