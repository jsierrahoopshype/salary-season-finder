    What the numbers say

    Mike Conley is the highest-paid Ohio State player ever, both in a single season ($34.5 million in 2020-21) and in career earnings ($301.5 million through 2026-27). He was the Jazz's highest-paid player in 2019-20 and 2020-21. He is fifth in career earnings among players who have not made an All-NBA team.
