What the numbers say

Mike Conley was the highest-paid player from the 2007 draft class in every season from 2016-17 to 2018-19. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $301.5 million, 32% of all the money ever paid to players out of Ohio State. He is fifth in career earnings among players who have not made an All-NBA team.
