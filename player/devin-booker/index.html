What the numbers say

Devin Booker has been the highest-paid player from the 2015 draft class every season since 2023-24. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $327.6 million, more than any other Kentucky guard. His $75.5 million in 2029-30 would be the biggest single-season salary of any player from the United States.
