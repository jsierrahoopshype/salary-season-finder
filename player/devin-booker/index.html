    What the numbers say

    Devin Booker's $57.1 million in 2026-27 is the highest single-season salary ever paid to a No. 13 pick. Donovan Mitchell is contracted to pass the single-season mark in 2027-28. He has been the league's highest-paid player from the 2015 draft class every season since 2023-24. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $327.6 million, more than any other Kentucky guard.
