    What the numbers say

    Chris Paul is the highest-paid player from the 2005 draft class ever, both in a single season ($41.4 million in 2020-21) and in career earnings ($404.3 million through 2025-26). Both are also records for a Wake Forest player, and his career total is the most ever by a No. 4 pick. He is fifth in career earnings among players who have not won MVP.
