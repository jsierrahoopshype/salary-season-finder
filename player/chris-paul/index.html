What the numbers say

Chris Paul was the highest-paid player from the 2005 draft class in every season from 2016-17 to 2020-21. He earned $404.3 million through 2025-26, more than any other No. 4 pick. He is fifth among players never named MVP, on $404.3 million paid to date.
