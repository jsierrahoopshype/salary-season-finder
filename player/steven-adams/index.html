What the numbers say

Steven Adams was the highest-paid No. 12 pick in every season from 2017-18 to 2020-21. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $199.1 million, more than every other player out of Pittsburgh combined. He is eighth among players yet to make an All-Star team, on $199.1 million paid to date.
