    What the numbers say

    Steven Adams is the highest-paid Pittsburgh player ever, both in a single season ($29.6 million in 2020-21) and in career earnings ($199.1 million through 2026-27). His career total is also the most ever by a No. 12 pick. He was the league's highest-paid player from Oceania in every season from 2017-18 to 2019-20. He is eighth in career earnings among players who have not made an All-Star team.
