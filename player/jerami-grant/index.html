    What the numbers say

    Jerami Grant has been the league's highest-paid Syracuse player every season since 2023-24. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $205.5 million, the second-most of any No. 39 pick. He is fifth in career earnings among players who have not made an All-Star team. His $36.4 million in 2027-28 would be the biggest single-season salary of any Syracuse forward.
