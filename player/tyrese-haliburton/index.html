    What the numbers say

    Tyrese Haliburton is the highest-paid Iowa State player ever, both in a single season ($48.9 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($154.5 million through 2026-27). His 2026-27 salary is also the highest ever for a No. 12 pick. He has been the Pacers' highest-paid player every season since 2024-25. His $55.7 million in 2028-29 would be the biggest single-season salary of any player from the 2020 draft class.
