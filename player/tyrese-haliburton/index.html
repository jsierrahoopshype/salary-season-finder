    What the numbers say

    Tyrese Haliburton has been the Pacers' highest-paid player every season since 2024-25. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $154.5 million, 3 times as much as the next player out of Iowa State, Kelvin Cato. His $55.7 million in 2028-29 would be the biggest single-season salary of any player from the 2020 draft class.
