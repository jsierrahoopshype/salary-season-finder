    What the numbers say

    Jaren Jackson Jr has been the league's highest-paid Michigan State player every season since 2025-26. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $194.6 million, the fourth-most of any player from the 2018 draft class. His $53.5 million in 2029-30 would be the biggest single-season salary in Jazz history.
