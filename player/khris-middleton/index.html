    What the numbers say

    Khris Middleton was the league's highest-paid second-round pick in every season from 2020-21 to 2022-23. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $298 million, 42% of all the money ever paid to No. 39 picks. He is fifth in career earnings among players who have not made an All-NBA team.
