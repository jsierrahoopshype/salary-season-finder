    What the numbers say

    Khris Middleton is the highest-paid No. 39 pick ever, both in a single season ($38 million in 2022-23) and in career earnings ($298 million through 2026-27). He was the league's highest-paid second-round pick in every season from 2020-21 to 2022-23. He is sixth in career earnings among players who have not made an All-NBA team.
