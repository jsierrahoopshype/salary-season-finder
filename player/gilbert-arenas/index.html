    Season by season, what changed

    Only the seasons that changed something, newest first.

    2013-14
    Raised the single-season record among players drafted outside the top 20 to $22.3 million. Got as high as No. 7 among players who have not won MVP.

    Became the first player on the Magic paid $20 million or more in a season.

    At $19.3 million he was the best-paid player from the 2001 draft class the league had seen, and a Magic record.

    Reached the NBA's top 10 salaries for the first time, eighth.

    2006-07
