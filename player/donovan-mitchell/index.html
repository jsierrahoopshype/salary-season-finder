    What the numbers say

    Donovan Mitchell is the highest-paid Louisville player ever, both in a single season ($50.1 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($238.7 million through 2026-27). He has been the Cavaliers' highest-paid player every season since 2025-26. His $78.8 million in 2030-31 would be the biggest single-season salary of any player from the United States.
