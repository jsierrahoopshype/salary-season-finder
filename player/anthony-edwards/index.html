    What the numbers say

    Anthony Edwards is the highest-paid Georgia player ever, both in a single season ($48.9 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($180.9 million through 2026-27). His career total is also the most ever by a player from the 2020 draft class. He has been the Timberwolves' highest-paid player every season since 2025-26. His $55.7 million in 2028-29 would be the biggest single-season salary of any player from the 2020 draft class.
