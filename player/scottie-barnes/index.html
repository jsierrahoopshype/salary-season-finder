What the numbers say

Scottie Barnes has been the highest-paid Florida State player every season since 2025-26. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $113.5 million, the second-most of any Florida State forward. His $51 million in 2029-30 would be the biggest single-season salary in Raptors history.
