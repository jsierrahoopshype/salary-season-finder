    What the numbers say

    Chandler Parsons is the highest-paid No. 38 pick ever, both in a single season ($25.1 million in 2019-20) and in career earnings ($127.2 million through 2019-20). Ayo Dosunmu is contracted to pass the single-season mark in 2030-31. He was the league's highest-paid player from the 2011 draft class in 2017-18 and 2018-19.
