What the numbers say

Chandler Parsons was the highest-paid No. 38 pick in every season from 2016-17 to 2019-20. He earned $127.2 million through 2019-20.

Season by season, what changed

Only the seasons that changed something, newest first.

2019-20
Set the biggest single-season salary in Hawks history, $25.1 million. Lost the single-season high among players from the 2011 draft class to Kemba Walker.
2018-19
Passed $100 million in career earnings. Climbed to third on the list of highest-paid Florida players ever.
2017-18
Moved to the top of the career-earnings list for players from the 2011 draft class. Climbed to fifth on the list of highest-paid Florida players ever.
