    What the numbers say

    Rudy Gobert is the highest-paid French player ever, both in a single season ($43.8 million in 2024-25) and in career earnings ($332.8 million through 2026-27). His career total is also the most ever by a No. 27 pick. Victor Wembanyama is contracted to pass the single-season mark in 2027-28. He was the Timberwolves' highest-paid player in every season from 2022-23 to 2024-25.
