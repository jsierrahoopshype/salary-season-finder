    What the numbers say

    Franz Wagner has been the Magic's highest-paid player every season since 2025-26. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $103.2 million, the third-most of any Michigan forward, behind Juwan Howard and Duncan Robinson. His $51 million in 2029-30 would be the biggest single-season salary of any No. 8 pick.
