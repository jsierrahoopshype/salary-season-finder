    What the numbers say

    Andrew Wiggins was the league's highest-paid Kansas player in every season from 2018-19 to 2022-23. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $281.6 million, more than any other player from Canada. He is seventh in career earnings among players who have not made an All-NBA team.
