    What the numbers say

    Andrew Wiggins' $281.6 million in career earnings through 2026-27 is the most ever by a Canadian player. He was the league's highest-paid Kansas player in every season from 2018-19 to 2022-23. He is seventh in career earnings among players who have not made an All-NBA team.
