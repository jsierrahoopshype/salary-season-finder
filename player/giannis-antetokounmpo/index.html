    What the numbers say

    Giannis Antetokounmpo is the highest-paid player from the 2013 draft class ever, both in a single season ($58.5 million in 2026-27) and in career earnings ($397.5 million through 2026-27). His 2026-27 salary is also the highest ever for a No. 15 pick or a Greek player, and his career total is the most ever by an international player. He was the Bucks' highest-paid player in every season from 2021-22 to 2025-26. His $62.8 million in 2027-28 would be the biggest single-season salary of any forward.
