    What the numbers say

    Giannis Antetokounmpo has been the league's highest-paid player from the 2013 draft class every season since 2021-22. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $397.5 million, more than any other international player. His $62.8 million in 2027-28 would be the biggest single-season salary of any forward.
