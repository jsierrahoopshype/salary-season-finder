    What the numbers say

    Bradley Beal was the league's highest-paid player from the 2012 draft class in every season from 2022-23 to 2024-25. By the end of 2026-27 he'll have earned $370.3 million, more than any other Florida player. He is eighth in career earnings among players who have not won MVP.
