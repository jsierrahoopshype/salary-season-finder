    What the numbers say

    Bradley Beal is the highest-paid Florida player ever, both in a single season ($50.2 million in 2024-25) and in career earnings ($370.3 million through 2026-27). He was the league's highest-paid player from the 2012 draft class in every season from 2022-23 to 2024-25. He is eighth in career earnings among players who have not won MVP.
