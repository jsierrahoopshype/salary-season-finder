    What the numbers say

    Dillon Brooks is the highest-paid Oregon player ever, both in a single season ($22.6 million in 2023-24) and in career earnings ($126.8 million through 2026-27). His 2023-24 salary is also the highest ever for a No. 45 pick. His $26.1 million in 2029-30 would be the biggest single-season salary of any No. 45 pick.
