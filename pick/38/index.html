Highest-paid No. 38 picks in the NBA

Chandler Parsons leads all No. 38 picks in career earnings since 1990-91, $127.2 million across 9 seasons. That is more than three times any other finished career in the group.

His $25.1 million in 2019-20 is also the biggest single season on the list.

In 2026-27 the highest-paid member is Ayo Dosunmu at $19.3 million.

Ayo Dosunmu's contract would pay him $20.9 million in 2027-28, $22.4 million in 2028-29 and $23.9 million in 2029-30, each one above anything the group has been paid.
