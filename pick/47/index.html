Highest-paid No. 47 picks in the NBA

Paul Millsap leads all No. 47 picks in career earnings since 1990-91, $193.5 million across 16 seasons. That is more than double any other finished career in the group.

His $30.8 million in 2017-18 is also the biggest single season on the list. That is more than three times any other member has been paid for a season.
