What the numbers say

  • Georges Niang's $9 million in 2025-26 is the highest single-season salary by a No. 50 pick since 1990-91, breaking his own mark of $8.8 million in 2023-24.
  • Georges Niang's $8.5 million in 2024-25 is the third-highest single-season salary by a No. 50 pick since 1990-91, behind his own $9 million (2025-26).
  • Georges Niang's $8.8 million in 2023-24 is the second-highest single-season salary by a No. 50 pick since 1990-91, behind his own $9 million (2025-26).
  • James Ennis' $14.6 million in career earnings through 2021-22 ranks fourth by a No. 50 pick since 1990-91, behind Ryan Gomes' $21.9 million (2013-14).
