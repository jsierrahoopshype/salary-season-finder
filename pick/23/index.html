Highest-paid No. 23 picks in the NBA

OG Anunoby has earned more than any other No. 23 pick, $180.5 million across 10 seasons to date. That is more than double what any other No. 23 pick has earned.

His $42.5 million in 2026-27 is also the biggest single season on the list. That is more than three times what any other No. 23 pick has been paid for a season.

OG Anunoby's contract would pay him $45.4 million in 2027-28 and $48.4 million in 2028-29, each more than any No. 23 pick has earned in a season.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
