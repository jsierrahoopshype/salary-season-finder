Highest-paid No. 44 picks in the NBA

Malik Rose tops No. 44 picks with $51.1 million through 2008-09.

That $7.6 million in 2008-09 is the biggest one year any No. 44 pick has had.

The money ahead is bigger still, with Pelle Larsson signed for $14.6 million in 2028-29, $15.7 million in 2029-30 and $16.3 million in 2030-31.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
