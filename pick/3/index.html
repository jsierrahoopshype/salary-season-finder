Highest-paid No. 3 picks in the NBA

$444.5 million through 2026-27 puts James Harden ahead of every other No. 3 pick.

$58.5 million in 2026-27 is the biggest single season any No. 3 pick has drawn, and it is Jayson Tatum's.

The money ahead is bigger still, with Jaylen Brown signed for $61.7 million in 2027-28 and $65.6 million in 2028-29.

Salary data starts in 1990-91; earlier salaries were far smaller in dollar terms.
