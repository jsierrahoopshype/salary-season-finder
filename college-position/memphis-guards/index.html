    Highest-paid Memphis guards in the NBA

    Derrick Rose is the highest-paid Memphis guard ever, both over a career ($167.7 million through 2023-24) and in one season ($21.3 million in 2016-17).

    Only Lester Quinones is still drawing an NBA salary, $2.5 million in 2026-27.
