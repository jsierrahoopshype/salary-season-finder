    Highest-paid Florida guards in the NBA

    Bradley Beal is the highest-paid Florida guard ever, both over a career ($370.3 million through 2026-27) and in one season ($50.2 million in 2024-25).

    He leads the 2026-27 payroll as well, $25.8 million.
